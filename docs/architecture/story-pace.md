### 3. Design — Short / Medium / Long

#### 3a. Two-layer budget

Each story length tier sets **two independent budgets**:

| Layer | What it is | How it's used |
|---|---|---|
| **Storybook text** | Narrative prose in the story body (SCENE 1..N) — shown as the story page | Rendered on screen; reader-facing. Sets the scene's "story weight". |
| **Narration script** | Voiceover spoken over the video clip | TTS → audio muxed over the scene clip. Must fit in clip duration. |

Derivation rule: **narration is written from the beat, not truncated from storybook text.** The scene director already has the beat (label + description); the narration planner gets the beat and a word budget.

At 2 words/sec (kids narration rate):
```
targetSec = ceil(narrationWords / 2) + breathingRoom   [breathingRoom: 1.5s–2.0s]
targetSec = clamp(targetSec, 4, 15)
```

Video duration is set to `targetSec`, not to a fixed 5s.

#### 3b. Revised targets

| Tier | Scenes | Narration words/scene | Scene duration (formula) | Typical dur/scene | Total runtime | Storybook words total | Storybook words/scene |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Short** (R16 default) | 3–4 | 12–25 | ceil(w/2)+2, clamp 4–15 | 8–14.5s | **30–45s** | 150–300 | 50–100 |
| **Medium** (non-R16 default) | 5–7 | 20–28 | ceil(w/2)+2, clamp 4–15 | 12–16s → capped 15s | **45–80s** | 400–700 | 57–140 |
| **Long** | chapters | per chapter as Medium | per chapter | per chapter | per chapter | per chapter | per chapter |

**Notes on Short:**
- 3 scenes × 12 words = 3 × (6+2)s = 24s; 4 scenes × 25 words = 4 × (12.5+2)s ≈ 58s → these are the extremes; typical Short is 3–4 × 18 words ≈ 35s. User target 30–45s is achievable.
- 12–25 words is a narrower target than the current 40–100. Narration prompt needs the explicit budget.

**Notes on Medium:**
- 5 scenes × 20 words = 5 × 12s = 60s; 7 × 28 words ≈ 7 × 16s = 112s → but 16s clips are clamped to 15s, so: 7 × 15s = 105s → over the 80s cap. To stay within 80s with 7 scenes: narration max ~24 words/scene. Use ceil(24/2)+2 = 14s. 7 × 14s = 98s — still high. **Tighten to max 6 scenes for Medium, or use 5–6 with a 20–25 word narration budget.** With 6 scenes × 25 words: 6 × (12.5+2)s = 87s — close to 80s. Targeting 5 scenes × 24 words = 5 × 14s = 70s or 6 × 20 words = 6 × 12s = 72s works. Use **5–6 scenes, 20–24 words narration** for Medium → **60–75s total**, which fits the 45–80s range.

#### 3c. Kids pacing rule (KIDS audienceMode)

Narration planner prompt addition for KIDS:
- One idea per scene, one emotion per scene
- No action beats back-to-back
- Simple vocabulary, max one clause per sentence

#### 3d. Beat-count constraint removal

Remove `Math.max(..., 6)` floor at `story.ts:4680`:
```typescript
// Before:
const sceneCount = educationalContractForScenes
  ? educationalContractForScenes.sceneProgression.length
  : Math.max(effectiveBlueprint.beats.length, 6);

// After (STORY_PACE_ENABLED):
const sceneCount = educationalContractForScenes
  ? educationalContractForScenes.sceneProgression.length
  : effectiveBlueprint.beats.length;  // floor = 3 (blueprint prompt min), ceiling = tier max
```

Blueprint prompt update: replace `"beats must have 4 to 6 entries"` with `"beats must have exactly ${beatCount} entries"`.

`buildSimpleScenes` fallback: parameterise count (default 6, Short=3, Medium=5).

If a beat needs more than ~15s narration: split into two beats during planning (blueprint prompt rule: "if a beat would require more than 25 narration words, split it into two beats").

#### 3e. Educational path

Keep `sceneCount = min(questions.length + 2, 6)`. Apply the same duration rule:
```
targetSec = ceil(narrationWords / 2) + 2, clamped 4–15
```
The `narrationText` is already in `educationalContractForScenes.sceneProgression[i].narrationText` (`types.ts:105`, max 600 chars). Word count from that drives duration.

#### 3f. Credits

MiniMax H3-Max Turbo (fal.ai) bills **per second of generated video**. No fixed per-clip overhead in the code. Total cost = total seconds × rate.

| Scenario | Clips | Duration/clip | Total video | vs. today |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Today (all stories)** | 6 | 5s | 30s | baseline |
| **Short (R16, new)** | 3–4 | 8–14.5s (avg ~10s) | 30–40s | ≈ same |
| **Medium (new)** | 5–6 | 12–15s (avg ~13s) | 65–78s | ~2.2–2.6x more |
| **Educational (new)** | 4 | 8–14s (avg ~11s) | 32–44s | ~1.1–1.5x more |

**Short costs about the same as today** (fewer clips, each longer). Medium costs 2–3x more per story — appropriate because it produces 2–3x more video. Document this in the UI: e.g. "Bigger story = more credits".

> **Verify:** confirm fal.ai billing is strictly per-second with no per-request floor. If there is a per-request floor (e.g. charge minimum 5s per call), fewer-but-longer clips may be meaningfully cheaper than 6 × 5s short clips. This should be confirmed before finalising the credit matrix.

### 4. Implementation sketch (Phase B)

All behind `STORY_PACE_ENABLED=true`. Staging first.

1. **Migration** — `storyLength` enum on `StoryProject`:
   ```sql
   CREATE TYPE "StoryLength" AS ENUM ('SHORT', 'MEDIUM');
   ALTER TABLE "StoryProject" ADD COLUMN "storyLength" "StoryLength";
   ```

2. **`createStory` / front-end** — `storyLength` input; defaults: SHORT for R16, MEDIUM for non-R16.

3. **Beat count from tier** — `SHORT → beatCount = 3–4`, `MEDIUM → beatCount = 5–6`. Pass to blueprint prompt (exact count), writer prompt (word budget), narration planner (narration words budget).

4. **Writer prompt update** — template `STAGE_1_SYSTEM` with `"${beatCount}-scene story, roughly ${wordsPerScene} words per scene"`.

5. **Blueprint prompt** — `"beats must have exactly ${beatCount} entries"`.

6. **`generateScenes`** — remove `Math.max(..., 6)`; use `blueprint.beats.length`.

7. **Duration from narration** — after `generateStoryNarration`, compute `targetSec` per scene from `narrationScript` word count; write to `project.productionManifest.scenes[].duration_sec`. `generateSceneVideo` already reads `manifestScene?.duration_sec` — no change there.

8. **Narration budget** — narration planner prompt gains explicit word budget: SHORT scenes = "12–20 words", MEDIUM scenes = "20–24 words".

9. **Fallback (`buildSimpleScenes`)** — parameterise count; SHORT = 3-scene variant, MEDIUM = 5-scene variant.

10. **Tests** — regression: `storyLength = null` → 6 scenes (existing behaviour unchanged). New: SHORT → 3–4 scenes + duration from narration; MEDIUM → 5–6 scenes + duration.

## Summary

The root cause is two independent constants that were never coordinated:

- **Scene count is fixed at 6** via `Math.max(beats, 6)` or `buildSimpleScenes`. The narrative engine's 3–5 scene prose has no influence on the 6 scene records created.
- **Duration is fixed at 5s** per scene regardless of narration length.

The "storybook text" (narrative prose body) and "narration script" (voiceover) are separate layers. The narration planner already targets 40–100 words per scene, but the video is only 5s (~10 spoken words) — the two are uncoordinated.

Phase B:
- Adds `SHORT` / `MEDIUM` to `StoryProject`
- Threads beat count through writer + blueprint + scene planner
- Computes per-scene duration from narration word count → writes to `ProductionManifest`
- Removes the `Math.max(..., 6)` floor

**Short costs about the same as today in video generation credits (30–40s total vs 30s); Medium costs ~2–3x more but creates a meaningfully longer story.**
